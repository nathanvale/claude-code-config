Floor-verb semantics × the ADR 0012 warning

How the browser-use facade floor should handle same-verb-different-behavior across engines without faking uniformity.

2026-06-12 repo-grounded · focused 7 survivors · 40 raw 5 frames · strong convergence feeds the facade-contract brainstorm
Strong convergence — the 40 candidates point at one answer. Across 5 independent frames (assumption-breaking, verify-as-truth, cross-domain analogy, constraint-flip, failure-modes), the dominant idea is the same: define floor verbs by their verified post-condition, not their mechanism — and make any behavioral divergence loud and typed at runtime rather than declared in a drift-prone manifest. This is simultaneously the most ADR-0012-honest answer (a post-condition that's checked can't be a "false capability claim") and the leanest (it reuses adapter_capability_partial and the verify loop the facade already wants). The remaining design space is a clean spectrum from a one-verb lean floor to a strict codec-normalized floor — and a named limit where verification can't reach.
The problem, concretely. Today a capability asserts a verb class exists (snapshot→snapshot_refs) and says nothing about behavior. But playwright's click auto-waits for actionability while chrome-devtools' CDP click is fire-and-forget at coordinates; playwright's snapshot is locator-based while chrome-devtools' is an a11y tree. Same verb name, different guarantee. ADR 0012 explicitly rejects a "universal browser API" that "makes false capability claims likely" — so the floor must deliver swappability without pretending these behave identically. WebDriver is the cautionary tale: one click across browsers still flaked, because the spec shared the verb name but under-specified timing.

Ranked ideas

★ 1

Postcondition floor — a verb is defined by what's true after, not how

Floor definitionConf HighCx M

The floor asserts nothing about mechanism — only the observable post-condition each verb must leave true. click(ref) floor = "after return, ref was actionable and an activation reached it." Engines reach that any way they like (native auto-wait, or fire-and-forget + poll). Swappability becomes outcome-equivalence, not mechanism-equivalence. Surfaced in 4 of 5 frames independently.

Basis · direct + reasoned

Today's capabilities "assert the verb CLASS exists; say NOTHING about behavior" (browser-adapter-router-engine.ts:81). Inverts the verb from mechanism to post-condition.

Why it matters

Resolves the ADR-0012 tension at the root: a post-condition is verifiable, so it can't be a false claim — if it holds, the floor held, regardless of engine. Test: can two reviewers independently write the same one-sentence post-condition for click without naming an engine? If yes, the floor is real.

Meeting test: "The floor doesn't say click waits — it says after click, the element was activated. Prove that or you didn't satisfy the floor."

★ 2

Verify is the leveler — proven, not declared

Verification as truthConf HighCx M

An engine doesn't declare floor-compliance; it proves it per operation. Every floor verb is operate → verify fused: the verb "succeeds at floor level" only if the bundled verify confirms the post-condition at runtime. There's no static manifest to lie, because the claim and its evidence are the same event. Directly operationalizes ADR 0012's "adapter reports and proof stay runtime-owned."

Basis · direct

ADR 0012: proof stays runtime-owned, skill prose names workflow not capability truth. The facade already wants verify-after-operate; this makes it the floor's enforcement mechanism, not a separate verb.

Why it matters

Drift becomes structurally impossible — there's nothing static to drift from. One generic verify-wrap replaces N per-behavior codec normalizations. The cleanest ADR-0012 fit available, and it's the natural twin of idea 1.

Meeting test: "There's no 'this engine waits' flag. There's only 'this operation verified its post-condition before returning.'"

★ 3

Loud typed divergence — reuse adapter_capability_partial, add behavior_realized

Divergence handlingConf HighCx S

Don't pre-declare behavioral flags (drift-prone — ADR 0012's rejected "static docs matrix"). Instead each verb result carries a typed behavior_realized field naming the guarantee actually applied this call (click{waited:false}), and when behavior falls below the reference form, emit the existing adapter_capability_partial diagnostic with evidence. The caller learns the real difference exactly when it's load-bearing, from runtime.

Basis · direct

Typed capability diagnostics already exist: adapter_capability_none/unknown/stale/partial (command-contract.ts:628). errno-style: the result carries its own cause class.

Why it matters

Kills the silent-flake failure: a fire-and-forget click is loud in the result, not buried under an identical verb name. Runtime diagnostics can't drift because they're emitted by the code path that has the behavior. Near-zero new surface — the leanest honest mechanism.

Meeting test: "When a click lands on a not-yet-actionable element, can the caller see in the result that no actionability wait happened — without reading engine docs?"

4

Facade-owned actionability loop — slow engine borrows the fast engine's guarantee

Codec burdenConf MedCx M

When an engine lacks native auto-wait, the facade — not each codec — wraps it in a verify-retry loop: snapshot actionability → dispatch → re-verify → retry-to-deadline. Playwright's native auto-wait and chrome-devtools-plus-facade-loop converge on the same post-condition. The normalization burden lives once in the shared layer, keeping codecs thin.

Basis · direct + reasoned

Concrete divergence: playwright auto-waits, CDP dispatchMouseEvent is fire-and-forget. Hoisting the loop to the facade is what makes "floor = behavior" (idea 1) achievable without heavy per-codec burden.

Why it matters

This is the bridge between lean and strict: it delivers real behavioral uniformity for the verbs that need it, without duplicating waiting logic in every adapter. Bound it by cost (idea 7).

Meeting test: "Run the same click against both engines under load — do both either succeed-with-actionable-target or fail-typed, with no silent coordinate-miss on chrome-devtools?"

5

The verify-blind limit — tier verbs by what verification can observe

Verification as truthConf HighCx M

The honest boundary of the whole approach. click/navigate are verify-checkable (DOM/URL state is observable post-hoc). But "did type fire real keystroke handlers?" and "did the page settle via network-idle?" are partially verify-blind — the post-condition isn't fully reconstructable. Classify each verb: verify-checkable verbs get the outcome-floor (ideas 1–2); verify-blind verbs fall back to a proxy post-condition (input value + framework-bound mirror updated), a measured flag, or explicit refuse-in-prose. And verify itself differs per engine (a11y-tree vs screenshot-diff), so verify_method must be typed — or verify becomes the new false-uniformity surface.

Basis · direct + reasoned

Named limit: a post-condition "can't always tell" if type fired handlers. Meta-divergence: "the verify loop itself differs per engine, so 'verified' means different things." testing-library precedent: assert observable consequence, not internal events.

Why it matters

Prevents over-claiming. Without this tiering, ideas 1–2 silently over-promise on verbs they can't actually verify. This is the guardrail that keeps the whole approach honest.

Meeting test: "For each floor verb, point at the concrete artifact verify reads to confirm the post-condition. Verbs with no such artifact are tagged verify-blind — no exceptions."

6

Conformance suite as admission gate (Khronos / Android CTS)

ADR-0012 fidelityConf MedCx L

An engine is admitted to the registry as floor-satisfying only by passing a behavior conformance suite — a tiny executable spec per verb ("does this click wait for actionability?"). Capability becomes a test verdict, not a hand-declared tri-state; a failing verb downgrades to adapter_capability_partial with the failing test as evidence. The suite is the floor definition, and admission is earned, slotting into the known→provable→mapped→selectable lifecycle.

Basis · reasoned

Khronos OpenGL/Vulkan CTS and Android CTS: "you may only call it conformant/Android if you pass the suite." Maps the existing confidence≥75 gate onto a proven-behavior verdict; feeds the existing evidence field.

Why it matters

The strongest anti-drift mechanism — behavior is proven per engine, re-runnable, not asserted. The natural way to make "wiring playwright" produce trustworthy capability claims. Heavier (Cx L), so a phase-2 move after ideas 1–3 land.

Meeting test: "When someone adds a new engine, what stops them from registering it as floor-complete without proving a single verb behaves?"

7

Refuse-the-route over silent-LCD (+ a refuse-in-prose roster)

ADR-0012 fidelityConf MedCx S

When a caller invokes a verb whose required behavior an engine can't deliver and can't codec-emulate, the contract refuses with a typed adapter_behavior_unmet naming the unmet guarantee — never silently executing the weaker version. Refusal is per-verb-per-behavior, so tasks that don't need waiting still route fine (avoids the over-strict-floor trap). Pair with a durable roster in SKILL.md naming guarantees deliberately left to agent judgment + prose — extending the existing "re-snapshot before element-ref actions" rule.

Basis · direct

ADR 0012 rejects "automatic fallback (silently leaves constraints)" — refusal is the honest alternative. SKILL.md already carries behavioral prose rules; the roster makes the refusal explicit and auditable.

Why it matters

Splits the false binary between "strict floor rejects engines" and "loose floor permits silent wrong results." Converts a silent omission into a deliberate, visible boundary — the thing reviewers actually fear is accidental false claims.

Meeting test: "Does a chrome-devtools click on a slow page get refused with a named reason, or executed as a coin-flip — and does a click on a known-stable element still route?"

Recommendation

The answer the convergence is handing you: define floor verbs by verified post-condition (idea 1) enforced by verify-after-operate (idea 2), with divergence surfaced as loud typed runtime signals reusing adapter_capability_partial + a new behavior_realized field (idea 3). Use the facade-owned actionability loop (idea 4) to let slow engines meet the post-condition. Bound the whole thing with the verify-blind tiering (idea 5) — the one guardrail that keeps it honest. Refuse-the-route (idea 7) handles the genuinely-unmeetable case. Defer the conformance suite (idea 6) to when playwright actually lands — it's the right anti-drift mechanism but heavier than the first cut needs.

This maps the three brainstorm options cleanly: it's mostly Option A done honestly (behavior is real, via verify + facade loop) but it avoids A's false-claim trap by making the guarantee checked, not declared — and it absorbs Option C's honesty (divergence is visible) without C's drift (signals are runtime, not manifest). Option B (pure LCD) survives only as the lean pole.

The WebDriver lesson to carry into the brainstorm: a shared verb name is not a shared behavior. The floor must spec the post-condition (timing, staleness) explicitly — that's the whole point — or it repeats WebDriver's cross-browser flake.
