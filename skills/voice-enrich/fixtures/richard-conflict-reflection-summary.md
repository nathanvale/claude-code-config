# Conflict Summary

Nathan and Richard had a tense exchange after Richard went quiet for nearly two weeks on Levi's birthday camping logistics. Nathan sent a frustrated message saying he felt like he was carrying the planning alone and did not know whether Richard actually wanted to come. Richard replied that the tone felt unfair and that he had been overwhelmed with work and family pressure rather than pulling away from Nathan.
